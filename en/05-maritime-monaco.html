Boat & Yacht Registration in Monaco

Registration under the Monegasque flag is reserved for Monegasque nationals and residents and is handled by the Direction des Affaires Maritimes. It requires documents that differ from the French system, such as photographs of the vessel and Monegasque tax compliance. We prepare and follow your file with the authorities of the Principality.

Our maritime services in Monaco:

  • Registration of new and second-hand vessels under the Monegasque flag.
  • Sale of a vessel registered in Monaco.
  • Crew and captain declaration.
  • Radio station licence for vessels.
  • Commissioning inspection, where required.
  • Annual naturalisation fee.
